¡Hola! Has llegado a mi página web personal.

Soy José Vila Ruiz, bibliotecario de metadatos y profesor asociado en la University of Miami, donde soy responsable de la unidad de Catalogación de Colecciones Interdisciplinarias dentro del Departamento de Servicios Técnicos. Mi trabajo se centra en la catalogación, la calidad de los metadatos, los sistemas bibliotecarios y el desarrollo de proyectos que mejoran el descubrimiento y el acceso a las colecciones de la biblioteca.

I am particularly interested in how library metadata is evolving beyond traditional record-based environments. My professional work and research explore BIBFRAME and linked data, emerging metadata workflows, and the practical application of artificial intelligence to cataloging, metadata quality control, and discovery.

My background is international: I was educated in Spain and the United States and participated in the Erasmus program in Belgium. I have presented and participated in professional activities across the United States, Latin America, and Europe, experiences that continue to shape my interest in multilingual collections, international librarianship, and collaboration across institutions and countries.

Metadata connects collections, technologies, and communities.
