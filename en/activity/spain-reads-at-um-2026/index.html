Exhibition · September 21–25, 2026

Spain Reads at UM

University of Miami Libraries · Miami, Florida

I organized Spain Reads at UM, an exhibition at University of Miami Libraries featuring a selection of books published in Spain from our collections.

To prepare the exhibition, I reviewed and selected books from a wide range of subject areas. I wanted to show that our collections of Spanish publications extend far beyond literature. They include works across many disciplines, reflecting the diversity of publishing in Spain and offering resources of interest to students and researchers in different fields.

The physical exhibition is accompanied by the Featured List “Spain Reads at UM” that I created in the library catalog. This list allows visitors to explore a broader selection of titles and continue discovering our collections beyond the books that could be displayed in the exhibition.

On Monday, September 21, I opened the exhibition alongside Mercedes Rosillo, Director of Cultural Industries at the Trade Office of the Embassy of Spain in Miami. We spent part of the morning talking with students and other visitors, introducing them to the selection and discussing the books, the Spain Reads at UM initiative, and the presence of Spanish publications in our collections.

I particularly enjoyed working on this project because it brings together several of my professional interests: Hispanic collections, the selection and acquisition of materials, improving their visibility and discoverability, and collaborating with other institutions to bring these collections closer to our university community.
