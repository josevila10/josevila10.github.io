La techne de la memoria: libros de artista como pensamiento material

Twenty-Fourth International Conference on New Directions in the Humanities · Nova School of Social Sciences and Humanities · Lisbon, Portugal

From July 1–3, I had the pleasure of attending the Twenty-Fourth International Conference on New Directions in the Humanities at the Nova School of Social Sciences and Humanities in Lisbon, Portugal. On July 3, I presented, together with my colleague Alejandra, our paper La techne de la memoria: libros de artista como pensamiento material.

The presentation explored how artists’ books transform poetry into a material and sensory experience, rather than simply preserving it as text. Focusing on poems by Cuban writers Dulce María Loynaz and Carilda Oliver Labra, we examined how the Cuban publishers Ediciones Vigía and El Fortín reinterpreted their poetry through artists’ books, giving the literary works a new visual, tactile, and material presence.

We also considered what happens when an artists’ book enters a library, particularly from the perspective of the cataloging librarian. Because these works blur the boundaries between books, artworks, and objects, cataloging them requires describing both their intellectual content and their distinctive physical and artistic characteristics. In this context, the cataloger becomes an important mediator between the work and its future readers.

I especially enjoyed participating in a conference centered on the humanities rather than on librarianship itself, as is the case with many of the professional events I usually attend. It offered a different context for discussing artists’ books and an opportunity to connect cataloging practice with broader conversations about literature, materiality, memory, and the humanities.
