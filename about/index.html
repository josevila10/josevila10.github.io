About

José Vila

I am a librarian and metadata professional working at the intersection of cataloging, linked data, emerging technologies, and access to library collections.

My professional work at the University of Miami Libraries has developed across cataloging, metadata management, library systems, and collections. I currently serve as Unit Lead for Interdisciplinary Collections Cataloging, with work that includes cataloging and metadata projects, metadata quality control, staff training, and collaboration across library units.

A central part of my work is exploring how library metadata can evolve beyond traditional record-based environments. My interests include BIBFRAME, linked data, Alma and discovery systems, and the practical application of artificial intelligence to cataloging and metadata workflows.

My professional perspective is also strongly international. I was educated in Spain and the United States, participated in the Erasmus program in Belgium, and have presented and participated in professional activities across the United States, Latin America, and Europe. This international dimension informs my interest in collaboration, multilingual collections, and the exchange of library practices across institutions and countries.
